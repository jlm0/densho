## Definition

The definition of the entities of a system, their attributes, and the relationships between them, in a form that a database stores. E. F. Codd's relational model of 1970 describes data as relations, which are tables of rows. Its aim is data independence: application programs do not depend on how the machine stores and organizes the data.
