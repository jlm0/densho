## Definition

The choice of a research method from the question that a study answers. Nielsen Norman Group places each method on the attitudinal and behavioral dimension, which separates what people say from what they do, and on the qualitative and quantitative dimension, which separates why and how to fix a problem from how many and how much. Early in product development, the methods are often generative, because they help a team decide which way to go.
