## Procedure

01. Collect the scope of the strategy: a product, a team, or a company, and the period that it covers.
02. Collect the facts of the situation: the users, the market, the competitors, the capabilities, the constraints, and the results.
03. Collect each goal or target that the strategy serves.
04. If a draft of the strategy exists, collect it.
05. If the diagnosis needs a fact that the inputs do not give, ask the person for it.
06. Write the diagnosis: the main challenge and the factors that make it hard.
07. Write the guiding policy: the approach that deals with the obstacles of the diagnosis.
08. State what the guiding policy rules out.
09. Write the coherent actions.
10. For each action, state how it supports the guiding policy and the other actions.
11. List the goals and the work that the strategy sets aside.
12. Check the draft for each sign of bad strategy that the page Strategy formulation names.
13. Correct each sign of bad strategy that you find.

## Output

One strategy document with 4 sections: the diagnosis, the guiding policy, the coherent actions, and what the strategy sets aside.
