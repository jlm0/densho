## Definition

A questionnaire of `10` statements that measures how people perceive the usability of a system, from John Brooke. The statements alternate between positive and negative, and each answer adds `0` to `4` points. The sum times `2.5` gives a score from `0` to `100`. Brooke warns that the score is not a percentage.
