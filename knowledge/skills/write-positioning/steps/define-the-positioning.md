## Procedure

01. Collect the product and its capabilities.
02. Collect the evidence from the customers who get the most value: interview notes, won deals, and lost deals.
03. If the product has a positioning, collect it.
04. Find the customers who get the most value from the product, and what they used before it.
05. List the competitive alternatives: what those customers do if the product does not exist.
06. List the unique attributes: what the product has that the alternatives lack.
07. For each attribute, write the value that it gives to customers.
08. Remove each attribute that gives no value.
09. Describe the target customers by the traits that make them care most about that value.
10. Choose the market category that makes the value obvious to the target customers.
11. Check each component against the others.
12. If a later component changes an earlier one, correct the earlier one.
13. After you correct a component, check each component after it again.

## Output

One positioning document with the `5` components in order, each with the evidence behind it.
