## Definition

A very simple program that explores a possible solution to one hard technical or design problem. Extreme Programming calls it a spike solution. The spike addresses only the problem under examination and ignores all other concerns. It reduces the risk of a technical problem, or makes the estimate of a user story more reliable. Extreme Programming puts a pair of developers on the problem for a week or two, and most spikes are thrown away.
