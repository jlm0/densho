## Definition

The four risks that Marty Cagan names for a product idea. Value risk asks whether customers will buy it or users will choose to use it. Usability risk asks whether users can work out how to use it. Feasibility risk asks whether the engineers can build it with the time, the skills, and the technology that they have. Business viability risk asks whether the solution also works for the business: its sales channel, its legal and partner constraints, the cost to acquire customers, its revenue, and its fit with the brand. The product manager owns the value and viability risks, the product designer owns the usability risk, and the lead engineer owns the feasibility risk.

## Notes

Cagan finds value risk the hardest in most cases. He added viability as a fourth risk because product managers tend to focus on customer value and overlook value to the business.
