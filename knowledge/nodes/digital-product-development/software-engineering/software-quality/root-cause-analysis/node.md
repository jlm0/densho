## Definition

The search for the cause of a problem behind its symptoms, so that the fix stops the problem from coming back. Taiichi Ohno of Toyota said: "Ask 'why' five times about every matter." In his example, a welding robot stopped because a fuse blew, and the fifth answer was a pump with no filter. The Lean Enterprise Institute notes that the number five is not the point: the team asks until it reaches the root cause.
