## Procedure

01. Collect the brand guidelines, the voice and tone guide, and the design token files.
02. If the brand has a list of distinctive assets, collect it with the measures of fame and uniqueness.
03. List the touchpoints in scope: screens, emails, advertisements, packaging, documents, and social media.
04. Collect a current sample of each touchpoint.
05. Compare the logo of each sample with the rules for its lockup, clear space, size, background, and effects.
06. Compare each color with the palette and the color roles.
07. Record each color that the palette does not hold.
08. Compare each text style with the type scale and the text styles of the guidelines.
09. Compare the voice and the tone of the copy with the guide for its type of message.
10. Check that each distinctive asset appears where the guidelines place it.
11. Record each rule that the guidelines lack, or that two parts of the guidelines state in different ways.
12. Group the findings by element.
13. Order the findings by the number of people who see each touchpoint.

## Output

A list of findings for each touchpoint, grouped by element.
