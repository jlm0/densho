## Definition

A way to specify a requirement through concrete examples that the team and the people who ask for the work write together. Gojko Adzic describes it as collaborative specifications and tests, and links it with agile acceptance testing and behavior-driven development. Each example is often a Given-When-Then scenario, a style from behavior-driven development by Daniel Terhorst-North and Chris Matts. Given states the world before the behavior, When states the behavior, and Then states the expected change.
