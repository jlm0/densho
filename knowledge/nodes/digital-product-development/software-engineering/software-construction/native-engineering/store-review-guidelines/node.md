## Definition

The rules that an app meets before an app store publishes it. Apple's App Store Review Guidelines have five sections: safety, performance, business, design, and legal, and experts review each app. The Google Play Developer Program Policies set the rules for content, privacy, and monetization that each app on Google Play complies with.
