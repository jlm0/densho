## Definition

Teresa Torres's method to evaluate an idea before a team builds it. An assumption is a belief that must hold for the idea to succeed. Assumptions fall into five types: desirability, viability, feasibility, usability, and ethical. An assumption test is a small, structured activity that evaluates the risk in one assumption, such as a prototype test, a one-question survey, a search of existing data, or an engineering spike. The riskiest assumptions are those that the idea depends on most and that have the least evidence. David Bland calls this ranking assumption mapping.

## Notes

Torres writes an assumption in the form that must hold, such as "users will remember their password", not its opposite. A test of what people do is easier than a test of what they do not do. A specific assumption gives a smaller and faster test.

An assumption test differs from an experiment such as an A/B test. An experiment measures the effect of something that the team already built. An assumption test helps the team decide what to build.
