## Definition

The work that decides what to build, before a team builds it. Discovery learns the needs of customers and tests ideas against the risks that they carry. Marty Cagan asks product teams to tackle the largest risks early, and to solve problems rather than deliver a list of features. Teresa Torres makes discovery continuous: the team interviews customers each week and tests the assumptions behind its ideas as it goes.
