## Definition

The mark that signs a brand. Alina Wheeler names its parts. The brandmark is the symbol. The logotype is the name of the brand set in a fixed form. The signature, also called a lockup, is the fixed arrangement of the brandmark, the logotype, and sometimes a tagline. A brand can allow a horizontal and a vertical signature, or a split signature in which the mark and the logotype appear apart. Logo standards set the clear space around the logo and its minimum size, so that the logo stays legible and stands apart from what is near it.

## Notes

A unit taken from the logo keeps the clear space in proportion at each size. Atlassian uses the height of the capital "A" of its wordmark. Apple sets the clear space around an App Store badge at one quarter of the badge height, and at one tenth where the layout has very little space. Apple sets a minimum badge height of `10 mm` in print and `40 px` on screen.

Atlassian gives each logo in three color options: brand, inverse, and neutral. GitHub uses its lockup of the mark and the wordmark in most places, and the mark alone where people already know the brand. Product lockups pair the parent mark with a product name.
