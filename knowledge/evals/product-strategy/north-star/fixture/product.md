# Pantry

Pantry is a meal planning app for households. Each week a household picks recipes,
and Pantry makes the shopping list and orders the food from a partner grocery shop.
A household pays a monthly subscription.

## What the team knows

- Households that cook 3 or more planned meals in a week keep their subscription for much longer.
- Many households sign up, plan one week, and then stop.
- The team can change the recipe suggestions, the planning screen, the shopping list, and the order flow.
