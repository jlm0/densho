# Fernway

Fernway sells hiking gear for people who walk in the hills at the weekend, not for expert climbers.

## Personality

- Fernway knows the outdoors well and shares what it knows without showing off.
- Fernway is warm and encouraging. It wants beginners to feel welcome on the trail.
- Fernway says what it means in a few words. Its readers check the website on a phone at the trailhead.
- Fernway takes safety seriously, but it does not lecture.

## Readers

Most readers are beginners who plan a day walk. Some readers are stressed because the weather changed or an order is late.
