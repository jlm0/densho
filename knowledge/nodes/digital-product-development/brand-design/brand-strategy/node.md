## Definition

The choices about what a brand means to the people it serves: its identity, the associations that it builds in their memory, and its place among the brands they compare it with. Brand strategy starts from the positioning of the product. The voice and the visual identity then express it.

## Notes

Kevin Lane Keller places the power of a brand in what customers have learned about it and experienced with it, so the measure of a brand is how customers think, feel, and act toward it. Alina Wheeler adds that a brand cannot be authentic until the organization is clear about its market, its positioning, its value, and its difference from competitors. That is why this practice area depends on the positioning practice area of product strategy.
