## Definition

The visual signs by which people recognize a brand: its logo, its colors, its typefaces, and its imagery. Alina Wheeler notes that the brain takes in shape first, then color, and only then the words, so these signs work before people read anything.

## Directives

- Remove each variant of a color, a typeface, or a logo that the brand guidelines do not list. ^remove-unlisted-variants

## Notes

The visual design practice area of UI/UX design holds the rules for a screen, such as the size of the palette on one interface and the number of text sizes on one screen. This practice area defines the brand values that those screens take. The contrast rules of the accessibility practice area apply to each pair of brand colors in text and in controls.
