## Definition

A model from research on reaction time: the time to make a decision grows with the number and the complexity of the choices. William Hick and Ray Hyman described it in 1952. The time grows with the logarithm of the number of choices, so the law matters most for short sets such as a navigation menu or a group of action buttons. It holds for choices of equal likelihood. A person who already knows what to pick decides faster than the law predicts.
