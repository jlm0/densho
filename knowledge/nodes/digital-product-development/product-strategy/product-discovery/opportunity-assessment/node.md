## Definition

Ten questions from Marty Cagan that a product manager answers before a company decides to build a product. They ask what problem the product solves, for whom, how large the opportunity is, which alternatives exist, why this company suits the work, why now, how the product reaches the market, how the company measures success, which factors are critical, and what the recommendation is. The assessment either stops work on a poor opportunity or shows what success requires.
