## Definition

A style of testing that runs a rapid cycle of learning, test design, and test execution, instead of a script written in advance. Martin Fowler contrasts it with scripted testing. Exploratory testing looks at the boundaries of the safety net of the automated tests, to find behavior that nobody expected.
