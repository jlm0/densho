## Definition

The house rules for the writing of an organization: its grammar, its mechanics, its terms, and its voice. GOV.UK keeps an A to Z style guide and a technical A to Z. The Mailchimp Content Style Guide covers the writing goals and principles, the voice and tone, and the grammar and mechanics.
