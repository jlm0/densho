## Procedure

1. List the types of message that the brand sends, and the emotional state of the reader for each one.
2. For each voice trait, list the reader states that increase it and the states that reduce it.
3. Write the tone guidance for each type of message, with one example message in the voice.
4. Write `3` samples of the current content again in the voice.
5. Compare each new sample with its original.
6. Write a test with users: they choose tone words for the samples, or rate them on each dimension.

## Output

The tone guidance for each type of message with its example, the `3` new samples, and the plan of the test with users.
