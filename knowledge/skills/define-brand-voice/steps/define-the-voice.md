## Procedure

1. If the brand has a positioning statement and an identity prism, collect them.
2. Collect the readers: who they are, and the emotional states in which they meet the content.
3. Collect samples of the current content: product copy, error messages, email, and marketing.
4. Describe the personality facet of the identity prism in human traits, with the five dimensions of Aaker.
5. Place the tone of the brand on each of the four tone dimensions.
6. Mark the default point and the range on each scale.
7. Choose the tone words and the anti-tone words.
8. Write the voice traits.
9. Give each trait a one-sentence explanation and a trait to avoid.

## Output

The personality, the tone profile, the tone words and the anti-tone words, and the voice traits with their explanations and the traits to avoid.
