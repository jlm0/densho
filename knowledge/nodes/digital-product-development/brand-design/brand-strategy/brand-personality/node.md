## Definition

The human traits that people link to a brand. Jennifer Aaker found five dimensions in a study published in 1997: sincerity, excitement, competence, sophistication, and ruggedness. Her scale measures each dimension, and she tested it for reliability, validity, and use across product categories.

## Notes

The personality facet of the identity prism describes the same thing from the side of the brand. The voice of the brand expresses it in words, and the visual identity expresses it in form. A voice trait such as "warm" or "direct" names how the personality sounds. Aaker's dimensions give a shared vocabulary to describe the personality before a team writes the voice.
