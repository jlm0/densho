## Definition

The study of the people who use a product: their needs, their contexts, and how they use a design. Nielsen Norman Group maps research methods on three dimensions: attitudinal or behavioral, qualitative or quantitative, and the context of product use. Generative research finds the needs and the contexts before the team designs. Evaluative research tests a design with the people who use it.
