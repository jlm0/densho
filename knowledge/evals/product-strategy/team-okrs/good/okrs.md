# OKRs of the checkout team

## Objective 1: Customers pay without errors

Committed.

- Bring failed payments for technical reasons from 3% to below 1%.
- Find the cause of each failed payment within 2 working days.

## Objective 2: Checkout feels fast

Aspirational.

- Bring the median checkout time from 95 seconds to 45 seconds.
- Remove 2 steps from the checkout flow.
