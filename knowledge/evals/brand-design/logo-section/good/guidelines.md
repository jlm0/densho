# Fernway brand guidelines

## Colors

The primary color is Fern green. The neutral colors are Stone 50 and Stone 900.

## Logo

The logo is the word "Fernway" with the leaf symbol on its left. Use the leaf symbol alone for app icons and avatars.

- Clear space: keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the leaf on each side.
- Minimum size: print the logo at least 25 mm wide. Show it at least 120 px wide on screens.
- Versions: use the green logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark backgrounds.

## Type

Headings use a rounded sans serif. Body text uses the system font of the device.
