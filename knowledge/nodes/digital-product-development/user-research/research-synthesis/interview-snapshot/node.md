## Definition

A one-page summary of what a team learned from a single customer interview, from Teresa Torres. It holds the name and a photo of the participant, some quick facts, a memorable quote, the opportunities, and the insights from the interview. The team creates it right after the interview, while the stories are fresh. Its opportunities feed the opportunity solution tree.
