## Definition

A field study that observes and interviews a small sample of users in the place where they do their work. Hugh Beyer and Karen Holtzblatt developed it, with the relationship between a master craftsman and an apprentice as its model. It does not rely on the memory of the user, unlike an interview or a survey. Its principles are context, partnership, interpretation, and focus.
