## Definition

The reference that states the rules of a brand and holds its assets for the people who apply them. The GitHub Brand Toolkit shows a common structure:

- Brand identity: the brand attributes, co-branding, and voice and tone
- Foundations: accessibility, color, logo, and typography
- Graphic elements: diagrams, iconography, illustration, layouts, mascots, product UI, and textures
- Brand in action: eBooks, events in person, presentations, social media, merchandise, and the web
- Motion identity: an overview, logo animation, and events

Alina Wheeler describes the shift from a static PDF to an online brand center that holds the assets and serves each group of users.

## Notes

The three labels appear in Wheeler's page on brand governance, next to a quote from Hampton Bridwell of Tenet Partners. The excerpt does not define them. A plain reading: a sacred element does not change, an interpretive element allows a stated range, and a customizable element is open to the team that applies it. The labels tell each user where the freedom is.

Atlassian shows each logo rule as a pair of a "Do" image and a "Don't" image. GitHub lists each misuse of its logo. An example of misuse shows the error faster than a rule in words.
