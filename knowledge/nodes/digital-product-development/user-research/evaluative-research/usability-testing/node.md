## Definition

A test in which representative users try to do real tasks with a design while a facilitator watches. In a thinking-aloud test, each user says out loud what they think as they work. Jakob Nielsen calls it the single most valuable usability engineering method. He finds the best results from tests with no more than `5` users, and as many small tests as the team can afford.
