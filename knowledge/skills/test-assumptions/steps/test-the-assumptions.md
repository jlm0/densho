## Procedure

1. Collect each idea, the opportunity that it addresses, and the outcome of the team.
2. If the opportunity or the outcome is missing, ask the person for it.
3. List the assumptions of each idea across the desirability, viability, feasibility, usability, and ethical types.
4. Choose the riskiest assumptions.
5. For each riskiest assumption, write a test and the result that counts as a pass.

## Output

A table of the assumption tests: the idea, the assumption, its type, the test, and the result that counts as a pass.
