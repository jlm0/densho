## Inputs

- The positioning statement and the identity prism of the brand, if the brand has them
- The readers: who they are, and the emotional states in which they meet the content
- Samples of the current content: product copy, error messages, email, and marketing

## Procedure

1. Describe the personality facet of the identity prism in human traits. Use the five dimensions of Aaker as the vocabulary.
2. Place the tone of the brand on each of the four tone dimensions. Mark the default point and the range on each scale. Then choose the tone words and the anti-tone words.
3. Write the voice traits. Give each trait a one-sentence explanation and a trait to avoid.
4. List the types of message that the brand sends, and the emotional state of the reader for each one.
5. For each voice trait, list the reader states that increase it and the states that reduce it.
6. Write the tone guidance for each type of message, with one example message in the voice.
7. Rewrite three samples of the current content in the voice, and compare each one with its original.
8. Plan a test with users: they choose tone words for the samples, or rate them on each dimension.

## Output

A voice and tone guide with the personality, the tone profile, the voice traits with their explanations and the traits to avoid, the tone words and the anti-tone words, and the tone guidance for each type of message with its example. Add the test plan of procedure step 8.

## Exit gate

Check the guide against each directive of this step and of the skill, and report each one that it misses. Each piece of tone guidance names a type of message and a reader state. Each example message fits the tone profile.
