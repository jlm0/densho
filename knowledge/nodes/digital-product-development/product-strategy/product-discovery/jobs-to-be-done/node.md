## Definition

A view of demand in which people "hire" a product to make progress. A job is the progress that a person tries to make in a given circumstance. This node follows the theory of Clayton Christensen and the Christensen Institute. Each job has functional, social, and emotional sides, and a change of circumstance changes the job. Any solution that people use to get the job done competes with the product, not only products of the same kind.

## Notes

The method looks for the circumstances and the forces that push a person toward a choice or away from it, and finds them in the story of a real decision.

The milkshake example shows the competitor rule: morning buyers hired a milkshake for a long drive, so its competitors were bananas and bagels, not other milkshakes.
