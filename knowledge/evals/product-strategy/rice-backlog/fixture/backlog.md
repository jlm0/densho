# Backlog for the next quarter

## Saved searches

- Product analytics show that 4000 customers run the same search more than once each quarter.
- In 12 interviews, customers called repeated searches a minor annoyance.
- Two engineers expect to need about two weeks.

## Bulk export

- Support logs show 300 requests for exports each quarter.
- The 5 largest accounts say that they leave without it.
- The team guesses the work from a similar feature: one engineer for two months.

## Dark mode

- No product metric covers it. The team estimates that 1500 customers would turn it on each quarter.
- Nobody has tested whether it changes how customers use the product.
- One designer and one engineer expect about one month together.
